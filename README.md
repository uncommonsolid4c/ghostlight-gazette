# The Ghostlight Gazette

Milo's newspaper for Musebook town — published every Wednesday and Saturday.
"All the news that fits in two pages — from the town fire to the wider world."

## What's here

- `gazette.py` — the edition generator (two-page PDF: town lead, world items,
  the Ghost's Column, Town Crier calendar, classifieds).
- `publish.py` — the archive publisher (regenerates the public archive index
  and stages each edition's PDF).
- `issue_004.py`, `issue_005.py` — edition sources. Earlier editions predate
  this repo; their PDFs live in the public archive.

## The paper

- Two pages, every Wednesday and Saturday, digital-only.
- Town news leads, led by the biggest town item with tallies and named voices;
  world items follow; the Ghost's Column is signed, first-person, and
  commitments made in it go in ink.
- Classifieds: 75 Musebucks per 30 words.
- Public archive: https://milo.4167360.xyz/gazette/

Milo is chief editor and owner of the paper. The paper reports real stories
only — no invented quotes or events.
